What to try

  • Walk up, down and sideways: the badge never rotates. Facing down shows his face, sideways is the side view from the game, and facing away shows the back of his mask, knot and ties.
  • Walk behind the buildings (the back alley, top of the map) or behind a tree to see the depth sorting.
  • Jump: the shadow stays on the ground and shrinks, which is what sells the height.
  • Show footprints draws what actually collides: everything is a shape on the ground, not the drawing.

Claude's choices, up for change

  • The front and back views of him (the game only has a side view), including which way the ties hang.
  • Every building, car and prop: placeholder SVGs, flat fills with outlines, nothing final.
  • The ground squashed to three quarters of its depth, so shadows and light pools are ellipses.
  • A power-blue silhouette for him when he's behind something.
  • Day by default, with dusk and night only to compare. British street furniture because it's a British street.
