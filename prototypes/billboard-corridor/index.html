What to try

  • Watch the guard in the alcove: it turns every few seconds, and its drawing switches between side and front as it does.
  • Let the patrolling guard walk past you, then follow it: front as it comes, side as it passes, back as it goes.
  • Walk round the pillar in the side room while the thief does his loop, through the lamp's light and out into the dark, where only his eyes are left.
  • Switch Look up and down to Free and look down at someone up close: that's when a billboard shows it's a flat card.
  • Punch: the floating gloves are your hands. In the dark they nearly vanish, which is the same open problem as in the stealth game.

Claude's choices, up for change

  • The watchman's front and back views (the game only has its side view), including how the cap's peak looks from each.
  • Billboards turn left and right only, like Daggerfall's and Doom's; looking up and down is limited by default.
  • The world's ink lines baked into its tiles, so they thin with distance the same way the characters' outlines do.
  • A clinical test-wing look: pale panels, a teal rail, pools of lamp light with dark stretches between.
  • A second copy of the thief, so you can see his billboard while you play as him.
